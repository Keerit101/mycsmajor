Small steps, every day

mycsmajor is built around one idea: a little practice every day beats a lot of practice the night before an exam.

Why one problem a day

Programming is a skill, and skills grow through regular practice. A single short problem is easy to fit between classes, and doing one every day adds up to hundreds of problems over a degree. The streak counter is there to make the habit stick.

Problems cover the three things CS courses and technical interviews ask about most: writing Python, reasoning through logic puzzles, and using data structures well. Each one has a hint and a full solution with an explanation, because seeing why an answer works is where most of the learning happens.

What else is here

  • Cyber Brief: the vulnerabilities being exploited right now, updated daily from CISA, plus the classic incidents explained.
  • Gear Guides: what matters in a laptop, keyboard and headphones, and what you can skip.

Our principles

  • Free to learn. Every daily problem, its solution and the free book chapters cost nothing and need no account. The full books are paid, and that is what keeps the rest free.
  • Private by default. Your streak lives in your own browser. We do not use tracking cookies. See the privacy policy.
  • Honest recommendations. Some gear links may earn us a commission, and we say so. It never decides what we recommend.
