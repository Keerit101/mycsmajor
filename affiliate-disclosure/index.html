Disclosure

Affiliate disclosure

Most of mycsmajor is free to use. The site earns money in three ways: paid books, affiliate links and, in future, ads. Here is exactly what each one means.

Last updated 24 October 2026.

Paid books

Some books on this site are sold. Every one of them has free chapters you can read first, and the free chapters are complete chapters rather than previews that stop early. Nothing you are told in a free chapter is shaped by what we would like you to buy.

Affiliate links

Some links in our Gear Guides are, or will become, affiliate links. If you click one and buy something, the shop may pay us a small commission. You pay the same price either way.

Every affiliate link is marked with rel="sponsored" in the page code, as search engines ask, and every page with buy links carries a short disclosure near them. At the time of writing, the buy links still point to the makers' own websites while affiliate programmes are set up.

How we choose what to recommend

  • We recommend things based on what matters for a computer science student: memory, battery life, keyboard comfort, noise and value.
  • A commission never decides whether something is recommended or where it appears on the page.
  • We name product lines rather than chasing every new model, and we tell you what you can safely skip buying.

Ads

The site does not show ads today. If that changes, ads will be clearly labelled "Advertisement", kept away from problem solutions, and the privacy policy will be updated before they appear.

What is never for sale

Daily problems, solutions, book chapters and Cyber Brief entries are written to teach. They do not contain paid placements.
