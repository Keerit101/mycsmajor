C and how memory really works

Pointers, the stack, the heap, and what your computer is actually doing.

C is small, unforgiving and the fastest way to understand what a program does to memory. This book teaches it with diagrams of the stack and the heap at every step.

Written alongside CPS 188 and CPS 305. 11 chapters. 2 of them are free to read on this site, starting with:

  • From Python to C: Compile and run your first program, and meet the compiler as a strict reviewer.
  • Pointers without fear: Addresses, dereferencing and the diagrams that make pointers obvious.
