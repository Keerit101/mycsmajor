Books for your first year

Seven short books covering what a first-year computer science student is actually asked to do. Every book has free chapters on this site, so you can read before you decide.

Each one is written the way these pages are: plain language, worked examples you can run, and exercises with answers you can check. No video courses, no subscriptions, no accounts.

Questions

What do I get when I buy one?

A PDF and an EPUB you download straight away and keep. No account, no app, and no expiry. Revisions are free.

Why are some chapters free?

So you can judge the writing before paying for it. The free chapters are complete chapters, not previews that stop at the interesting part.

Can I share it with my class?

Buy one copy per person, please. The books are cheap for exactly that reason, and the free chapters are there for anyone to read.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If a book is not what you expected, reply to the receipt within 30 days and you get your money back.
