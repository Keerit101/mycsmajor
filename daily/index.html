    How it works

    • A new problem appears at midnight in your time zone. Days rotate between Python, cyber security and data structures, and each track gets a little harder every time it comes round, over a 90-day cycle.
    • Press "I solved it" once you have a working answer. Solving on back-to-back days builds your streak; missing a day resets it.
    • Your streak is saved only in this browser using localStorage. There are no accounts and nothing is sent to us. Clearing your browser data or switching devices starts you from zero.
