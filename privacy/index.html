Cookies and local storage

mycsmajor.com does not set cookies and does not use analytics, advertising or social media trackers. The only thing the site stores on your device is your streak, in localStorage under the key mycsmajor:streak:v1, and only after you choose to press "I solved it". It is needed for a feature you asked for, never leaves your device, and you can delete it any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Because nothing optional is stored, the site does not show a cookie banner. If we ever add analytics or ads, this page will be updated first and, where the law requires it, we will ask for your consent before anything is set.

Checkout pages, tip pages and other sites we link to are run by others and may use their own cookies under their own policies.

Buying a book or leaving a tip

Checkout happens entirely on Stripe's own pages, not on mycsmajor.com. We never see or store your full card details. Stripe processes your payment and your data under its own privacy policy, and sends us your email address, name, country, what you bought and the amount.

When you open the download page, mycsmajor asks Stripe whether your order is paid and records how many times each book has been downloaded, in Stripe. Nothing about your order is stored on mycsmajor.com.

To protect each licence, the provider may print your email address or name into the files you download and may limit how many times a download link works. That information is used only to identify the licensed copy.

Who else processes data

  • Vercel hosts the site and processes technical data to deliver pages.
  • Stripe processes payments and holds order records.
  • Our email provider stores messages you send us.

These companies may store data outside Canada, including in the United States, where it can be subject to local law. Fonts are served from this site, not from a third-party font service.

Your rights

Under Canada's PIPEDA, and the GDPR or UK GDPR if you are in Europe or the UK, you can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted (except records the law makes us keep). Europeans can also object to processing, ask for a copy in a portable format, and complain to their local data protection authority. In Canada you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

For Europe and the UK, our legal bases are: performing a contract (delivering what you bought), legal obligation (tax records), and legitimate interests (replying to you, keeping the site secure).

To use any of these rights, email hello@mycsmajor.com. We reply within 30 days.

Security

The site is static: there are no accounts, passwords or databases on mycsmajor.com to break into. Every page is served over HTTPS only, with strict security headers. If we ever learn of a breach that affects your information and creates a real risk of significant harm, we will tell you and the regulator as the law requires.

Children

The site is aimed at university and college students. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you think a child has sent us information, email us and we will delete it.
